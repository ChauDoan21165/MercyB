My typical Sunday is simple but comfortable. I usually get up around eight because I do not have to hurry for work. In the morning, I have coffee with my parents and we talk about the week. Sometimes we go to the market to buy food for lunch. My mother cooks, and I help her wash vegetables, although I am not very good at cooking. In the afternoon I meet some friends at a cafe or stay home to watch a movie. Sunday helps me feel relax before Monday starts again.
