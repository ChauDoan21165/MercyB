Sundays unfold with a deliberately unhurried rhythm: coffee at my regular spot near Hoan Kiem, a slow walk through streets that feel briefly returned to the people who live in them, and lunch with my parents if everyone is free. I rarely plan much beyond that. Sometimes I see a film; sometimes I read for an hour and end up thinking instead. By evening I make a loose peace with the week ahead, answer the messages I have been postponing, and let the day close without trying to make it productive. It is the one small luxury I protect.
