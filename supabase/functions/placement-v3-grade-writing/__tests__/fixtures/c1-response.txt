My Sundays tend to move at a deliberately slower pace than the rest of the week. I usually start with coffee and a long walk, partly to clear my head and partly because Hanoi feels more generous before the traffic becomes impossible. Lunch is often with my parents, which has become less of an obligation than a small ritual I would miss if it disappeared. In the afternoon I might read, see a film, or catch up with a friend, depending on how demanding the previous week has been. What I value most is the permission Sunday gives me to be useful to no one for a few hours.
