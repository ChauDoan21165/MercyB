My Sundays are usually quiet, but I try to keep them meaningful. I wake up later than usual, make coffee, and spend the first hour reading or reviewing the plans I ignored during the week. Around noon I often meet my parents for lunch, either at home or at a small restaurant near the market. In the afternoon I go for a walk around Hoan Kiem Lake with a friend, and we talk about work, family, and whatever is happening in the city. By evening I prepare for Monday, but I leave some time for a film or a long call with my brother. It is not an exciting day, yet it gives me the balance I need.
