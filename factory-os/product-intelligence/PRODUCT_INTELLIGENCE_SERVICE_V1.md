# Product Intelligence Service v1

## Mission

Product Intelligence Service observes Teacher Mercy Intelligence product runtime behavior and produces Product Observations.

It does not create engineering work.

## v1 Component

Runtime Event Observer.

## Product Runtime Flows

- placement
- tutor
- speaking
- listening

## Output

ProductObservation objects that can later feed:

Product Observation
→ Engineering Observation
→ Engineering Investment
→ Engineering Objective
→ Engineering Execution Contract
→ Factory
→ Independent Judge

## Boundary

This v1 installs the durable observer model and testable runtime event observation contract.

It does not claim runtime readiness.
It does not claim any product capability is verified.
