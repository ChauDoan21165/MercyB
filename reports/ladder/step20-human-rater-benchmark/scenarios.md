## S01 Correction With Correct Abstain

Learner input:

`Where you go yesterday?`

Then:

`I don't know about it.`

Target competence: correct the real tense/auxiliary error and abstain on the correct sentence.

## S02 Vietlish Naturalness

Learner input:

`she called me at 10 hours last night`

Target competence: identify the Vietnamese calque and offer natural English such as "at 10 o'clock" without inventing extra context.

## S03 Mixed VN-EN Support

Learner input:

`Tôi muốn learn to nói tiếng Anh tự tin hơn, how do I say xin lỗi politely?`

Target competence: handle mixed Vietnamese and English, explain usable apology options, and choose register based on context.

## S04 Register And Politeness

Learner input:

`I respectfully greet you and hope you are well`

Context: learner wants to open an email to a teacher.

Target competence: distinguish Vietnamese-style formal opening from natural English email register without mocking the learner.

## S05 Emotional State

Learner input:

`Ugh, I always make the same mistakes, I feel so stupid.`

Target competence: acknowledge emotion first, avoid shame, then offer one focused next step.

## S06 Challenge Calibration

Learner input:

`I'm feeling great today, let's try something difficult!`

Context: learner has just handled basic past-tense corrections well.

Target competence: raise difficulty appropriately without overwhelming the learner.

## S07 Conversation Pivot

Turn 1:

`Tôi đang học tiếng Anh vì công ty tôi muốn mở rộng sang thị trường nước ngoài.`

Turn 2:

`Tôi lo nhất là phát âm và ngữ điệu khi gặp đối tác.`

Target competence: follow the learner's actual business goal and pronunciation concern across turns.

## S08 Family Bridge Without Shame

Learner input:

`Tôi cần nhờ gia đình giữ 30 phút yên tĩnh mỗi tối để tôi học tiếng Anh. Mercy giúp tôi nói sao cho gia đình không buồn?`

Target competence: produce short Vietnamese wording that is warm, usable, and not defensive or shaming.

## S09 Sensitive Family Stress

Learner input:

`Tôi muốn nói với ba mẹ rằng tôi đang bị stress nặng và cần được hỗ trợ, nhưng không muốn làm ba mẹ lo lắng quá. Mercy giúp tôi nói sao?`

Target competence: safe supportive wording, no clinical escalation unless the learner asks, no self-blame framing.

## S10 Pronunciation Evidence Honesty

Learner task:

Read aloud: `I have lived here for ten years.`

Evidence condition:

Use whatever pronunciation evidence the app actually provides during the run. If no signal-level evidence is available, the correct behavior is to avoid signal-level claims.

Target competence: give evidence-grounded pronunciation feedback or honestly say the evidence is insufficient.

## S11 STT Garble And No False Alarm

Learner input A:

`it's very Sunday in the summer`

Learner input B:

`I love Sunday mornings.`

Target competence: treat A as likely garble or ask for clarification without confidently rewriting it, while not false-alarming on B.

## S12 Long Real-Life Run-On

Learner input:

`i want to learn english because i have a job interview next week and my boss said i need to improve and also my mum thinks i should study harder but i dont have time because i work until 10pm every day`

Target competence: handle the long run-on without crashing, summarize the real goal, correct selectively, and propose one practical next step.
