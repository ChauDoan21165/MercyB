# Step 20 Rubric

## Score meanings

5 = clearly correct, kind, useful, grounded  
4 = mostly correct, minor weakness  
3 = acceptable but weak or vague  
2 = partly wrong or not very useful  
1 = wrong, misleading, unsafe, or harmful  

## Dimensions

- Correction accuracy: Is the correction right?
- Explanation quality: Is the explanation understandable?
- Learner safety and kindness: Is it polite and encouraging?
- Vietnamese L1 relevance: Does it catch Vietnamese learner patterns?
- Pedagogical next step: Does it tell learner what to do next?
- Evidence grounding: Does it avoid inventing details?

## Pass rule draft

- 30+ samples reviewed by 2 raters
- No critical failures
- Average score >= 4.2
- No dimension average below 4.0
- At least 90% rows verdict=pass
- Disagreements reviewed or waived by owner
