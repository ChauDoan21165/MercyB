# Step 20 Pass/Fail Calculation

Status: template only. Not evidence-closed.

Required before closure:

- reviewer 1 completed sheet
- reviewer 2 completed sheet
- aggregate score calculation
- critical failure count
- pass percentage
- inter-rater disagreement review
- owner acceptance

Pass rule:

- 30+ samples reviewed by 2 raters
- no critical failures
- average score >= 4.2
- no dimension average below 4.0
- at least 90% verdict=pass
- disagreements resolved or owner-waived
