# Vietnamese -> English Interference Notes - Education & School Administration

**Author:** A5
**Scope:** education and school-administration interactions: enrolling a child or adult
learner, asking about tuition and fees, speaking with front-office staff, emailing a
teacher, booking or attending a parent-teacher meeting, clarifying forms, deadlines,
documents, attendance, transfers, and support services.
**Audience:** Vietnamese parents and adult learners who need clear, respectful English
with school offices, teachers, registrars, counselors, and program coordinators.

Companion to `interference-notes-school-education.md`. That broader note covers school
vocabulary, parent-teacher communication, homework, grades, and academic writing. This
note narrows the lens to the **school-admin register**: the moments where a learner must
ask about enrollment, payment, policy, documentation, and meetings without sounding
either too blunt or too apologetic.

Same contrastive format: **VN** = Vietnamese source or mental model; **ENx** = typical
learner output; **EN✓** = natural English target.

MercyBlade principle: interference is **friendly context, never a scolding**. The goal is
a calm adult voice that can ask school staff for help, confirm facts, and protect the
family's interests honestly.

---

## 1. Domain-Specific Interference Map

Vietnamese school-admin language is shaped by hierarchy, indirectness, and the habit of
treating the school as an authority. English-speaking school offices are usually more
process-oriented: staff expect direct questions, exact names, dates, documents, and
follow-up requests.

High-frequency interference patterns:

1. **Role address transfers too literally.** `Thưa cô`, `thưa thầy`, `chị ơi`, and
   `anh ơi` carry politeness in Vietnamese. English needs a greeting, a name if known,
   and the request. Bare `Teacher` or `Sister` sounds wrong.
2. **"Register" is overused.** Vietnamese `đăng ký` maps to several English verbs:
   `enroll`, `register`, `sign up`, `apply`, `book`, and `complete a form`.
3. **Fees are not only "school money."** `Học phí`, `lệ phí`, `phí ghi danh`, `tiền ăn`,
   and `tiền xe đưa đón` need different English labels: `tuition`, `fee`, `registration
   fee`, `meal fee`, `transportation fee`.
4. **Vietnamese omits articles and possessives.** English school-admin speech needs
   `the form`, `a copy`, `my child's record`, `the tuition payment`, `her teacher`.
5. **Indirect politeness can hide the actual ask.** Long Vietnamese-style openings may
   delay the request. English office staff appreciate a short context sentence followed
   by a direct question.
6. **Honesty matters more than fluency.** Do not invent rules, prices, eligibility, or
   timelines. Teach learners to say what they know, what they do not know, and what they
   need the school to confirm.

## 2. Register With School Staff

### 2.1 Openers: Respectful Without Over-Performing Formality

Vietnamese formal openings often sound ceremonial when translated word-for-word.

- VN: *Kính thưa cô, em muốn hỏi về hồ sơ đăng ký học cho con.*
  ENx: "Respectfully teacher, I want ask about register study for my child."
  EN✓: **"Hello Ms. Tran, I have a question about enrolling my child."**

- VN: *Chị ơi, cho em hỏi học phí tháng này.*
  ENx: "Sister, I ask tuition this month."
  EN✓: **"Hi, I have a question about this month's tuition."**

- VN: *Dạ em làm phiền một chút được không ạ?*
  ENx: "Can I bother you a little?"
  EN✓: **"Do you have a moment?"** / **"Could I ask a quick question?"**

Teaching point: English politeness comes from **greeting + modal + clear request**, not
from translating every Vietnamese respect particle.

### 2.2 Titles And Names

Safe defaults:

| Situation | Natural English |
|---|---|
| Teacher, name known | **Ms. Tran**, **Mr. Lee**, **Dr. Patel** |
| Office staff, name unknown | **Hello**, **Hi**, **Excuse me** |
| Email to teacher | **Dear Ms. Tran,** |
| Email to office | **Hello,** / **Dear School Office,** |
| After staff signs with first name | **Hi Sarah,** is usually acceptable |

Avoid:

- ENx: "Teacher, my son is sick." -> EN✓ **"Hello Ms. Tran, my son is sick today."**
- ENx: "Madam teacher" -> EN✓ **"Ms. Tran"**
- ENx: "Sister in office" -> EN✓ **"the office staff"** / **"the receptionist"**

### 2.3 Formal But Not Submissive

Vietnamese parents may feel they must apologize repeatedly. English-speaking schools
usually expect partnership.

- ENx: "I am very sorry to disturb you many times."
  EN✓: **"Thank you for your help. I have one follow-up question."**

- ENx: "If the school allows, I would like to ask..."
  EN✓: **"Could you please let me know whether this is possible?"**

- ENx: "I don't dare to decide."
  EN✓: **"I want to confirm the policy before I decide."**

## 3. Enrollment And Registration

### 3.1 `Đăng ký học`: Choose The Right Verb

`Đăng ký` is not always `register`.

| VN intent | EN target | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Xin vào trường hoặc chương trình | **apply** | "How do I apply for this program?" |
| Chính thức cho con vào học | **enroll** | "I'd like to enroll my child." |
| Điền tên vào lớp/sự kiện | **sign up** | "Can I sign up for the evening class?" |
| Hoàn tất thủ tục với văn phòng | **register** | "We need to register before the first day." |
| Đặt lịch gặp | **book / schedule** | "Can I schedule a meeting with the teacher?" |
| Điền giấy | **complete / fill out a form** | "Do I need to fill out this form?" |

Examples:

- VN: *Tôi muốn đăng ký học cho con.*
  ENx: "I want to register study for my child."
  EN✓: **"I'd like to enroll my child."**

- VN: *Đăng ký lớp tiếng Anh buổi tối ở đâu?*
  ENx: "Where register evening English class?"
  EN✓: **"Where can I sign up for the evening English class?"**

- VN: *Con tôi có được nhận vào trường này không?*
  ENx: "My child can enter this school?"
  EN✓: **"Is my child eligible to attend this school?"** / **"Can my child apply to this
  school?"**

### 3.2 Documents And Forms

Common nouns:

| VN | EN |
|---|---|
| hồ sơ đăng ký | **application / enrollment documents** |
| giấy khai sinh | **birth certificate** |
| hộ chiếu | **passport** |
| giấy tờ cư trú | **proof of address / proof of residency** |
| sổ tiêm chủng | **immunization record / vaccination record** |
| học bạ | **school record / transcript** |
| người liên hệ khẩn cấp | **emergency contact** |
| người giám hộ | **guardian** |
| mẫu đơn | **form** |
| bản sao | **copy** |

Article practice:

- ENx: "I bring birth certificate."
  EN✓: **"I brought my child's birth certificate."**

- ENx: "Do you need passport?"
  EN✓: **"Do you need a copy of the passport?"**

- ENx: "I don't have immunization."
  EN✓: **"I don't have the immunization record with me today."**

Honesty rule: if a document is missing, do not pretend it exists. Use:

- **"I don't have that document with me today. Can I bring it tomorrow?"**
- **"I'm not sure which document you need. Could you show me the list?"**
- **"I need to check whether this record is up to date."**

### 3.3 Dates, Deadlines, And Intake

Vietnamese learners often say `deadline nộp hồ sơ` as a noun phrase without the English
question frame.

- ENx: "Deadline application when?"
  EN✓: **"When is the application deadline?"**

- ENx: "Can my child start from next Monday?"
  EN✓: **"Could my child start next Monday?"**

- ENx: "School receive student in middle year?"
  EN✓: **"Do you accept students mid-year?"**

- ENx: "If late, still can register?"
  EN✓: **"If we miss the deadline, is there a late registration option?"**

Teach both date formats because forms vary:

- **June 12, 2026** = common North American style.
- **12 June 2026** = common in many Commonwealth and international-school contexts.

## 4. Tuition, Fees, And Payment

### 4.1 `Học phí` Is Tuition, But Not Every Cost Is Tuition

| VN | EN target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| học phí | **tuition** | Cost of instruction or program |
| lệ phí / khoản phí | **fee** | General fee |
| phí ghi danh | **registration fee / enrollment fee** | May be one-time |
| phí nộp hồ sơ | **application fee** | Paid to apply, not a guarantee of acceptance |
| tiền đặt cọc | **deposit** | May or may not be refundable |
| tiền ăn | **meal fee / lunch fee** | School meal cost |
| tiền xe đưa đón | **transportation fee / bus fee** | School bus/shuttle |
| phí đồng phục | **uniform fee** | Uniform cost |
| phí hoạt động | **activity fee** | Clubs/events/materials |
| học bổng | **scholarship** | Awarded support |
| hỗ trợ tài chính | **financial aid** | Need-based support |
| trả góp | **payment plan / installments** | Scheduled payments |
| hoàn tiền | **refund** | Money returned |

Examples:

- VN: *Học phí một tháng bao nhiêu?*
  ENx: "Tuition one month how much?"
  EN✓: **"How much is the monthly tuition?"**

- VN: *Có phải đóng thêm tiền ăn không?*
  ENx: "Need pay more food money?"
  EN✓: **"Is the meal fee included, or is it separate?"**

- VN: *Phí ghi danh có được trả lại không?*
  ENx: "Registration money can return?"
  EN✓: **"Is the registration fee refundable?"**

### 4.2 Honest Fee Questions

School fees can be confusing and high-stakes. The learner should not guess.

Good questions:

- **"Could you give me a breakdown of the fees?"**
- **"Which fees are required, and which are optional?"**
- **"Is tuition paid monthly, by semester, or annually?"**
- **"Are there any late fees?"**
- **"Is there a payment plan?"**
- **"When is the next payment due?"**
- **"Could you send the fee schedule by email?"**

Avoid false certainty:

- ENx: "I know this fee includes everything."
  EN✓: **"Could you confirm what this fee includes?"**

- ENx: "The website says no pay deposit."
  EN✓: **"I may have misunderstood the website. Is a deposit required?"**

- ENx: "You promised refund."
  EN✓: **"I was told the deposit may be refundable. Could you confirm the refund policy?"**

### 4.3 Payment Methods

Useful frames:

- **"What payment methods do you accept?"**
- **"Can I pay by card?"**
- **"Can I pay by bank transfer?"**
- **"Do you accept cash?"**
- **"Could I get a receipt?"**
- **"Can you email me the invoice?"**
- **"What should I write in the payment reference?"**

VN -> EN pitfalls:

- VN: *Chuyển khoản nội dung ghi gì?*
  ENx: "Bank transfer content write what?"
  EN✓: **"What should I put in the payment reference?"**

- VN: *Cho tôi xin hóa đơn.*
  ENx: "Give me invoice."
  EN✓: **"Could I please get an invoice?"**

- VN: *Tôi đã đóng tiền rồi.*
  ENx: "I paid money already."
  EN✓: **"I've already paid."** / **"The payment has already been made."**

## 5. Meeting A Teacher Or School Administrator

### 5.1 Booking The Meeting

`Gặp giáo viên` may mean a quick word, a scheduled conference, or a formal appointment.
English needs the meeting type.

- VN: *Tôi muốn gặp cô giáo của con.*
  ENx: "I want meet teacher of my child."
  EN✓: **"I'd like to schedule a meeting with my child's teacher."**

- VN: *Có thể gặp cô lúc tan học không?*
  ENx: "Can meet teacher when school finish?"
  EN✓: **"Would it be possible to speak with Ms. Tran after school?"**

- VN: *Tôi cần gặp hiệu trưởng.*
  ENx: "I need meet principal."
  EN✓: **"I'd like to request a meeting with the principal."**

Register ladder:

- Quick informal: **"Do you have a minute after class?"**
- Standard: **"Could we schedule a time to talk?"**
- Formal/escalated: **"I'd like to request a meeting with the principal or program
  coordinator."**

### 5.2 Explaining The Reason

English staff will often ask, "What is this regarding?" Learners need one clear sentence.

Useful reasons:

- **"It's about my child's progress in reading."**
- **"It's about the tuition payment schedule."**
- **"It's about enrollment documents."**
- **"It's about a concern my child raised at home."**
- **"It's about support for English-language learning."**
- **"It's about a possible transfer to another class."**

VN pitfalls:

- ENx: "I want talk private thing."
  EN✓: **"I'd like to discuss a personal matter about my child."**

- ENx: "My child has problem with friend."
  EN✓: **"My child is having a problem with a classmate."**

- ENx: "Teacher has time for me?"
  EN✓: **"When would be a good time for the teacher?"**

### 5.3 During The Meeting

High-value phrases:

- **"Could you explain what happened?"**
- **"Could you give me an example?"**
- **"What can we do at home to help?"**
- **"What will the school do next?"**
- **"Could we agree on a follow-up date?"**
- **"I want to make sure I understood correctly."**

Face-saving but honest:

- VN: *Chắc do con tôi chưa ngoan, mong cô bỏ qua.*
  ENx: "Maybe my child not good, please forgive."
  EN✓: **"I understand the concern. We'll talk with him at home, and we'd like to work
  with you on a plan."**

- VN: *Tôi không hiểu lắm nhưng ngại hỏi.*
  EN✓: **"I'm not sure I understood. Could you explain that another way?"**

## 6. Email And Written Notes

### 6.1 Email Template: Enrollment Question

```text
Hello,

I have a question about enrollment for my child. My child is currently in Grade 3, and
we would like to know whether there are spaces available for the next school year.

Could you please let me know what documents are required and when the application
deadline is?

Kind regards,
[Parent name]
```

### 6.2 Email Template: Tuition Or Fee Question

```text
Hello,

I have a question about the tuition and fees for this program. Could you please send me
the current fee schedule and let me know whether tuition is paid monthly or by semester?

I would also like to know whether the registration fee is refundable.

Kind regards,
[Name]
```

### 6.3 Email Template: Meeting Request

```text
Dear Ms. Tran,

Could we schedule a time to talk about Minh's progress in reading? I would like to
understand how he is doing and what we can do at home to support him.

Please let me know what time works best for you.

Kind regards,
[Parent name]
```

Teaching note: `Kind regards` is safe and professional. `Best regards` is also fine.
Avoid over-formal closings if the rest of the email is simple.

## 7. Grammar Targets For This Domain

### 7.1 Articles

- ENx: "I filled form." -> EN✓ **"I filled out the form."**
- ENx: "I need appointment." -> EN✓ **"I need an appointment."**
- ENx: "Can I speak to teacher?" -> EN✓ **"Can I speak to the teacher?"**
- ENx: "School office sent email." -> EN✓ **"The school office sent an email."**

### 7.2 Prepositions

- **enroll in** a program / school / class
- **apply to** a school; **apply for** a scholarship
- **pay for** tuition / lunch / transportation
- **meet with** a teacher
- **speak to** the office
- **ask about** fees / documents / deadlines
- **eligible for** financial aid
- **due on** Friday; **due by** June 30

Examples:

- ENx: "I apply scholarship." -> EN✓ **"I applied for a scholarship."**
- ENx: "I enroll my son in Grade 2." -> EN✓ **"I enrolled my son in Grade 2."**
- ENx: "Payment due in Friday." -> EN✓ **"The payment is due on Friday."**

### 7.3 Tense And Proof Of Payment

- VN: *Tôi đã gửi giấy tờ hôm qua.*
  ENx: "I send documents yesterday."
  EN✓: **"I sent the documents yesterday."**

- VN: *Tôi đã đóng học phí rồi.*
  ENx: "I pay tuition already."
  EN✓: **"I've already paid the tuition."**

- VN: *Tôi chưa nhận được email.*
  ENx: "I not receive email yet."
  EN✓: **"I haven't received the email yet."**

### 7.4 Questions

- ENx: "Need bring what document?" -> EN✓ **"What documents do I need to bring?"**
- ENx: "Tuition include lunch?" -> EN✓ **"Does tuition include lunch?"**
- ENx: "Teacher available when?" -> EN✓ **"When is the teacher available?"**
- ENx: "Can refund or not?" -> EN✓ **"Is it refundable?"**

## 8. Honesty Rule For School-Admin Content

School-admin language can affect money, enrollment, immigration-adjacent paperwork,
child safety, and access to support. The tutor must not hallucinate school policy.

Use these patterns:

| When the learner asks... | Honest response pattern |
|---|---|
| "How much is tuition?" | **"You need to check the school's current fee schedule."** |
| "Can my child enroll?" | **"Eligibility depends on the school. Ask the office what documents and age/grade rules apply."** |
| "Is the fee refundable?" | **"Refund rules vary. Ask for the refund policy in writing."** |
| "Can I pay late?" | **"Do not assume. Ask whether there is a grace period or late fee."** |
| "Do I need vaccination records?" | **"Requirements vary by location and school. Ask for the document checklist."** |
| "Can I record the meeting?" | **"Ask for permission first. Recording rules vary."** |

Preferred tutor language:

- **"I can help you phrase the question, but the school must confirm the policy."**
- **"Let's ask for the fee schedule in writing."**
- **"Let's not guess. Say: 'Could you confirm that by email?'"**
- **"If this affects money or enrollment, ask for the exact deadline and amount."**

Avoid:

- Saying a fee is included unless the school has confirmed it.
- Promising a refund, placement, transfer, scholarship, or payment extension.
- Telling the learner to omit missing documents.
- Softening a serious concern so much that the school cannot act.

## 9. Repair Phrases When The Learner Is Stuck

These phrases should be drilled because they let learners stay honest under pressure.

- **"Sorry, could you repeat that more slowly?"**
- **"Could you write that down for me?"**
- **"Could you send that by email?"**
- **"I want to make sure I understood correctly."**
- **"Let me check my documents and get back to you."**
- **"I don't have that information with me right now."**
- **"Could you tell me the next step?"**
- **"Who should I contact about this?"**
- **"Is there someone who speaks Vietnamese, or can I bring an interpreter?"**

VN-to-EN register note: asking for help understanding is not a loss of face in this
context. It is responsible parent/admin behavior.

## 10. Drill Set For Future Speak Topics

1. **Enrollment call:** parent asks whether Grade 4 has space, what documents are needed,
   and when the deadline is.
2. **Fee breakdown:** adult learner asks whether tuition includes books, meals, transport,
   and registration.
3. **Payment plan:** parent asks whether monthly installments are available and when the
   first payment is due.
4. **Missing document:** learner does not have the immunization record today and asks when
   they can bring it.
5. **Teacher meeting:** parent books a meeting about reading progress and asks what they
   can do at home.
6. **Office clarification:** learner receives a form they do not understand and asks the
   office to explain the next step.
7. **Refund policy:** learner asks whether the deposit is refundable before paying.
8. **Class transfer:** adult learner asks if they can move to a different level because
   the current class is too easy or too difficult.
9. **Absence and makeup work:** parent explains an absence and asks what work the child
   needs to make up.
10. **Support services:** parent asks whether English-language support, counseling, or
   learning support is available.

Each drill should enforce:

- one clear opening,
- one exact school-admin noun,
- one honest "please confirm" move,
- full Vietnamese diacritics in VN-facing explanations,
- and no invented policy, price, deadline, or eligibility claim.
