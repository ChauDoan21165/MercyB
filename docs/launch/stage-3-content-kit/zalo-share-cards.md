# Zalo share cards — Stage 3 launch

Five one-liner share-card texts for Zalo. Each is short enough to read on a phone preview without scrolling, written for a Vietnam-resident audience.

## Why Zalo gets its own format

Zalo behaves differently from Facebook and TikTok:

- **External links get their preview cards stripped.** Pasting `mercyblade.com/weak-at` into a Zalo message often shows the URL as plain text, not a rich card with thumbnail + headline. That kills the affordance Facebook posts rely on.
- **Audience is Vietnam-resident.** Bilingual VI + EN dilutes; VI-only is correct.
- **Forward-and-forward dynamics dominate.** A share that survives 3 forwards has to read clean each time — no context-dependent setup, no "this only makes sense if you read my previous post".
- **Message length tolerance is short.** Long-form is read on Facebook; on Zalo, one line + a CTA is the unit.

Each card here is **< 100 characters in the hook line + a separate CTA line**. Send them as two-line messages with a manual line break, OR as a single line with the URL appended.

## The five cards

Copy the text inside `---` blocks. Each card is named for its angle so you (or a future operator) can pick by purpose.

---

### Card 1 — "the diagnostic ask"

```
Bạn có biết mình hay mắc lỗi tiếng Anh gì không?
MercyBlade tổng hợp 3 điều — không cần đăng nhập.
👉 mercyblade.com/weak-at
```

**Use when**: opening a conversation with someone who's said something like "tôi học tiếng Anh hoài mà vẫn không khá". Anchors the gap as "biết là gì" — a knowable thing — rather than a vague struggle.

**Use when**: forwarding to friends/family who are mid-journey with English and report feeling stuck. The "một chạm" framing makes it sound low-friction, which it is.

## What survives forwarding

If only one card can be forwarded to a wide list, pick **Card 1** — it carries the broadest diagnostic ask without requiring the receiver to know anything about MercyBlade beforehand.

Card 3 is the second-best for forwarding into general Zalo groups, because the "no streak / no XP" framing is the clearest differentiator a stranger can grasp in one glance.

Cards 2, 4, and 5 reward more context — keep them for one-to-one messages or topical groups.

## What NOT to do on Zalo

- **No emoji-stuffing.** One 👉 is enough. Three or more reads as forwarded-chain-mail.
- **No URL shorteners.** Zalo's filter sometimes flags shortened URLs as spam-suspect. The mercyblade.com URL is short enough.
- **No images attached to a card.** Zalo image + text behaves inconsistently across versions; the text-only card is the reliable format. (If an image is desired, send the screenshot as a separate message before the text card.)
- **No tagging in groups.** Tagging strangers in a Zalo group is socially abrasive in a way it isn't on Facebook. The card has to earn the click on its own.
- **No "share đi" / "forward đi" pleas.** Forward-begging dilutes the value and reads as MLM-tone.

## What this is NOT

- These cards are NOT cold-outreach scripts. They are share cards — designed for people who already have a reason to be talking with the recipient. Cold DMs to strangers using these cards is spam and will get the sender banned.
- These cards do NOT replace a Facebook post. They forward the link; the Facebook post does the explaining for an audience that wants to read more.
- These cards do NOT claim outcomes. None of the five says "you will improve / fluency guaranteed / pass IELTS". A learner clicks, looks, decides.
