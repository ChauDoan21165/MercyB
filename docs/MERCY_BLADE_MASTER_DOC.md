# Mercy Blade Master Doc

## Mercy Blade

Mercy Blade is a bilingual English learning app for Vietnamese learners.

It helps people learn English through real thoughts, real feelings, real daily life, and meaningful ideas, not shallow drills.

The app combines:
- English + Vietnamese support
- audio-first learning
- structured progression from Level 0 to Level 9
- Teacher Mercy as the main study guide
- a kids picture-speaking library for ages 3–7

---

## Core Idea

Mercy Blade helps users learn English through meaning.

Instead of memorizing random sentences, learners study ideas, events, feelings, reflections, and real-life experiences. The goal is not only better English, but clearer thinking and more natural expression.

---

## Main Feature: Teacher Mercy

Teacher Mercy is the heart of the app.

She encourages users to write about:
- daily life
- real events
- feelings
- thoughts
- things that impressed them most

Then she helps step by step:

- correct the writing
- explain grammar
- improve the sentence naturally
- help the user practice speaking the corrected version
- analyze the English logic
- help the user avoid Vietlish

She also guides the learner to stay with the idea that impressed them most, study that idea more deeply, and remember it better.

Teacher Mercy is not just a correction tool. She is a study companion who helps users turn real life into English practice.

---

## Library

The Library lets users explore rooms from Level 0 to Level 9.

In the rooms, users can:
- read
- listen
- reflect
- study ideas more deeply

The rooms are designed for meaningful learning, not fast scrolling.

---

## Kids Feature: Picture Library with Teacher Mercy

Mercy Blade also includes a picture-speaking library for children ages 3–7.

Children can:
- pick a picture
- hear Mercy speak
- speak back
- record their own voice
- compare their voice with Mercy
- get a speaking score
- see encouraging animation

This gives young children a simple, playful, and safe way to begin speaking English.

---

## What Makes Mercy Blade Different

### 1. Real-life English
Users write about their real life, not fake textbook situations.

### 2. Teacher-guided improvement
Teacher Mercy helps with writing, grammar, speaking, and English logic in one connected flow.

### 3. Anti-Vietlish focus
The app does not stop at grammar correction. It also explains English logic so users can sound more natural.

### 4. Meaning before memorization
Users learn best from what truly impressed them, felt important to them, or happened in their real day.

### 5. Kids and adults in one system
Adults can study through reflection and writing. Children can study through pictures, listening, speaking, and encouragement.

### 6. Level-based growth
The app now uses Level 0 to Level 9 progression.

## Who Mercy Blade Is For

Mercy Blade is for:
- Vietnamese learners who want more natural English
- people who want to write about real life and get guided correction
- learners who want help with grammar, speaking, and English logic together
- children ages 3–7 who learn well through pictures and imitation
- parents who want a safe and structured English tool for kids
